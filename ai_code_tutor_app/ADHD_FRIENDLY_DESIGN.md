# ADHD-Friendly Learning Design Notes

The app is designed around a simple principle: the learner should never have to decide from a blank page what to do next.

## What changed in v7

- **Today tab** gives one mission, one review queue, one official AI side quest, and one end-of-session check-in.
- **Focus Coach** adds rescue mode, body-double scripts, low-stimulation preferences, break reminders, a parking lot, and focus check-ins.
- **Projects tab** turns lessons into small capstone checkpoints instead of one intimidating final project.
- **Gamification** rewards proof of learning: completed lessons, quizzes, daily missions, prompt practice, focus check-ins, official AI resources, and project milestones.
- **Shame-free reset design** keeps skipped days from destroying the learning path. A skipped day moves the learner forward; completed days build streaks.

## Daily 30-minute learning loop

1. Brain dump or warm-up.
2. Tiny start.
3. Ten-minute sprint.
4. Reset break.
5. Ten-minute practice/build sprint.
6. Save proof of understanding.

## Why this matters

Common friction points for ADHD learners include time blindness, distractibility, difficulty starting, working-memory overload, and all-or-nothing thinking. The app responds by making the next action visible, keeping sessions short, externalizing memory, and creating low-pressure ways to resume after missed days.

This app is not a medical tool or treatment plan. It is a coding-study interface that tries to be more forgiving, structured, and motivating.
